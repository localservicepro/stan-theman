About us

Your Local, Fully Insured Exterior Cleaning Team

Stan The Man Cleaning is a genuinely local business, built on hard work, reliability and a real pride in the results we deliver across Melbourne's Eastern Suburbs.

Stan The Man Cleaning was founded by Stan Krejnus with a simple goal: to give homeowners a cleaning service they can actually rely on. Based in Donvale, we've grown by word of mouth — one spotless job at a time — because we treat every property as if it were our own.

When you book with us, you're not dealing with a faceless franchise or a rotating cast of contractors. You're dealing directly with an owner-operator who turns up when he says he will, does the job thoroughly, and stands behind the work. That personal accountability is the difference you can see in every clean.

What we stand for

We keep things straightforward: honest pricing, punctual arrivals, careful work and a tidy finish. We're fully insured and use proper safety equipment on every job, so whether we're up a ladder clearing your gutters or on the roof cleaning solar panels, you have complete peace of mind. And because we offer the full range of exterior cleaning under one roof, you can sort your whole property with a single, easy phone call.

Local knowledge that matters

The Eastern Suburbs have their own quirks — leafy streets that fill gutters fast, dusty summers that dull solar panels, and cool, wet winters that leave paths slippery with algae. As locals, we understand exactly what your home is up against and how to keep it looking its best all year round.
