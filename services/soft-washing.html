Soft Washing Melbourne

Soft Wash House Washing in Melbourne

The gentle way to a spotless exterior. Soft washing safely lifts mould, mildew and algae from render, cladding and rooflines — without the risk of high-pressure damage.

Get a Free Quote Book Online

Not every surface can handle a blast of high pressure. Render, painted cladding, weatherboards and roof tiles can be damaged by aggressive cleaning — which is exactly why soft washing exists. Using low pressure and specialised, biodegradable cleaning solutions, soft washing gently breaks down mould, mildew, algae and grime at the source, then rinses it away for a clean, even finish that lasts.

Stan The Man Cleaning offers professional soft washing across Melbourne's Eastern Suburbs. It's the safest way to refresh your home's façade and roofline, restoring that clean, cared-for look without putting your surfaces at risk.

What's included

  • Low-pressure washing of house exteriors and façades
  • Render, cladding, weatherboard and painted surfaces
  • Roof soft washing to remove moss, lichen and algae
  • Eaves, fascias and gutters exterior cleaned
  • Biodegradable, surface-safe cleaning solutions
  • Even, streak-free finish that lasts longer than a rinse

Why soft washing beats high pressure on delicate surfaces

High pressure can crack render, strip paint, force water under cladding and dislodge roof tiles. Soft washing avoids all of that. Because it treats the organic growth chemically rather than just blasting the surface, it also keeps mould and algae away for longer — so your home stays cleaner between visits. It's the smart, gentle choice for rendered homes, painted exteriors and roofs across the Eastern Suburbs.

Soft washing questions

Soft washing uses low pressure — similar to a garden hose — so it won't damage delicate surfaces. It usually involves biodegradable, environmentally friendly cleaning solutions applied before a gentle low-pressure rinse, which break down and remove organic growth like algae, moss and mould.
